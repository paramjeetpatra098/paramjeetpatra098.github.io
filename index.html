HOW I WORK

Technology is the input. Outcomes are the point.

My product approach combines customer evidence, technical depth and measurable business outcomes.

01

Start with the workflow

I look for the job customers are trying to complete before deciding what technology or feature to build.

02

Make the technical trade-off explicit

For AI, APIs and data products, I work closely with engineering to understand constraints, reliability, scalability and failure modes.

03

Design for adoption

A launch is not the finish line. Discovery, onboarding, customer education and usage loops are part of the product.

04

Measure business impact

I connect product decisions to adoption, usage, productivity, revenue and customer value wherever the signal is measurable.
